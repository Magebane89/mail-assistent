Mail-Assistent

An email assistant that runs on your own computer. It categorizes incoming messages with a local AI model, summarizes each one in a sentence, and finds invoices and receipts.

What the app does

  • Reads new messages from your Gmail or Outlook mailbox, with read-only access.
  • Assigns each message to a category, such as project inquiry, accounting, appointment or newsletter, and writes a short summary.
  • Flags invoices and receipts as candidates for your bookkeeping.
  • Shows everything in an overview in your browser that is reachable only from your own computer.

How it handles your data

  • Everything stays on your computer. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
  • The AI runs locally. A language model on your computer does the analysis. Email content is not sent to any AI service on the internet.
  • Read-only access. The app cannot change, delete or send anything in your mailbox.
  • Revocable at any time. You disconnect a mailbox with one click in the settings, or in your Google or Microsoft account.

The details are in the privacy policy.
