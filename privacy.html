Mail-Assistent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: Mail-Assistent runs entirely on your own computer. Your emails are read, analyzed and stored there. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.

1. Who is responsible, and contact

Mail-Assistent is a private, non-commercial software project. The developer answers privacy questions at gitihubert@gmail.com.

You install and run the app yourself. You are responsible for the data it processes on your computer.

2. Which data the app reads

When you connect a mailbox, the app reads the following through the provider's interface:

  • sender, subject, time received and text of your emails,
  • names, types and sizes of attachments,
  • the content of a message or an attachment when you create or forward a receipt from it,
  • the email address of the connected account.

The app never sees your password. Sign-in happens directly between your browser and Google or Microsoft.

3. What the data is used for

  • To assign each message to a category and summarize it in a sentence.
  • To detect invoices and receipts.
  • To show you the result in an overview on your computer.

The analysis is done by a language model that runs locally on your computer. The data is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models. No human other than you reads it.

4. Where the data is stored

Only on your computer, in the app's folder:

  • a local database with sender, subject, time received, a text excerpt, attachment names and the category of each message,
  • generated receipt PDFs and saved original messages, if you use that feature,
  • the access token that allows the app to read your mailbox.

The overview is reachable only from your own computer and is protected by a password.

5. Sharing with third parties

The app does not share data with the developer or with third parties. There is one exception, which you have to turn on yourself: forwarding receipts to your bookkeeping software. It is off by default, requires an additional permission to send email, and sends a receipt only when you approve that single receipt with a click. The receipt is sent from your own mailbox to the address you entered yourself.

6. Data from Google accounts

For Gmail, Mail-Assistent uses the gmail.readonly permission and, only when receipt forwarding is turned on, additionally gmail.send.

Mail-Assistent's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data from Microsoft accounts

For Outlook and Hotmail mailboxes, Mail-Assistent uses the Microsoft Graph permission Mail.Read. The same rules apply as above: read only, analyze locally, no sharing.

8. Retention, deletion and revoking access

  • The data stays stored until you delete it. Deleting the app's folder is enough.
  • In the app's settings you disconnect a mailbox with one click. The stored access token is deleted in the process.
  • Independently of that, you can revoke access with the provider at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Microsoft at account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access.

9. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and contains no analytics or advertising services. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when the page is loaded. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

10. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects how it processes data, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
